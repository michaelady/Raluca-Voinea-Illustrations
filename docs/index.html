About Raluca

Romanian illustrator, painter, and graphic designer based in Neuchâtel, Switzerland — working in Romanian, English, and French. Her work moves between warm portraiture, children’s book covers, floral studies, and tributes to artists she admires.

Follow new illustrations on Instagram, explore the Artweb gallery, or browse prints on Society6.

Her studio is in Neuchâtel. She takes illustration and graphic-design commissions for clients in Switzerland and online.
