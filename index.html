About Raluca

Painter and illustrator based in Neuchâtel, Switzerland. Her portfolio spans dreamlike atmospheres, figurative studies, and tributes to artists such as Hilma af Klint, Niki de Saint Phalle, Tamara de Lempicka, and Yayoi Kusama.
